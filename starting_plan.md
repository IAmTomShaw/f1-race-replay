## The project in 5 sentences

1. After each practice and qualifying session, we predict how fast each car will be in Sunday's race, and how sure we are.
2. We save that prediction publicly **before** the next session, so nobody can say we changed it later.
3. After the race we score ourselves: were we right, and were our "how sure" ranges honest?
4. We turn the prediction into decisions a team needs: how long each tyre lasts, when to pit.
5. Then we explain where the time went, and add an AI assistant you can ask questions.

Everything else (data pipelines, tables, tests, the app) exists to make those 5 things work and be trusted.
